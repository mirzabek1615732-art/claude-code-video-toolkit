# Mirzabek Reels Playbook

Personal IELTS blog on Instagram. Goal for now: views and followers. Goal later: online students.
Written 16 Nov 2026. Future editing chats should read this file first and follow the rules in section 3.

---

## 1. Niche: "The IELTS teacher who shows proof"

**Positioning:** an Uzbek IELTS 8.0 teacher who explains English the way Uzbeks get it wrong, and shows real student results.

Why this niche and not a general lifestyle blog:

- **It already worked.** Your intro reel got 10k views (boosted with an ad, so the organic number was lower). Its strongest moments were proof: "14 yoshli o'quvchim IELTS 8.0", your 9.0/8.5/7.0/7.0 scores, a student going 5.5 → 7.5. Keep that.
- **Pure "my life" vlogs grow slowly** unless you're already known. "You + one clear topic" grows faster, because people know why to follow. (From your "Opening Instagram" chat.)
- **IELTS is a mass topic in Uzbekistan,** and Uzbek-speaker-specific mistakes are something a general English account can't copy. That makes your Reels original, and Instagram's 2026 updates push original content over reposts.
- **It leads to students later** without changing anything. Followers who trust your results become leads.

### Content pillars (rotate them)

| Pillar | Share of posts | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Uzbek mistakes** | 35% | Reach. People send these to friends, and sends matter most for reaching new viewers. | "10 ta o'zbekdan 9 tasi shu xatoni qiladi" |
| **B. Quiz / test yourself** | 20% | Comments and rewatches | "Bu savolga 10 soniyada javob bering" |
| **C. Student results** | 20% | Trust (and later, sales) | "5.5 → 7.5. U nimani o'zgartirdi?" |
| **D. Myth-busting** | 10% | Saves and debate | "8.0 uchun qiyin so'zlar shart emas" |
| **E. Personal blog** | 15% | Connection: makes people follow *you* | "IELTS ustozining bir kuni", Webster TESOL life |

**Trends this fits (Nov 2026):** talking-head Reels are still recommended for trust, *but only if the first second shows something concrete*. A slow intro loses. "Structured authenticity" is the current trend: timestamped routines, emotionally framed transformations, and step-by-step processes. Pillar C is a transformation and pillar E is a timestamped routine. These come from marketing blogs, not Meta's own data, so treat them as direction, not fact.

## 2. Weekly plan

- **4 Reels a week**, for example Mon A, Wed B or D, Fri C, Sun E. Plus **Stories daily** (behind the scenes, polls).
- **Length:** 20–35 s for pillars A, B and D; up to 45 s for C and E.
- **Use Trial Reels** for new formats. They're shown to non-followers first, and you get the numbers after about 24 h.
- **What to judge by, after 30 days:** watch time, *sends* and saves. Not likes. Read the organic and ad numbers separately; a boosted reel's views say little about the content.
- Film 4 videos in one sitting (same light, same shirt for a series) to save time.

## 3. Rules collected from your earlier chats (apply to every video)

From the **Instagram video analyzer** chat (Episode 1 lost half its viewers by 7 s, 0 follows from 1,265 views, payoff too late):
1. **Result or hook in the first 2 seconds.** Never start with "Assalomu alaykum, men…".
2. **Hook text on the very first frame,** large, inside the safe zone.
3. **Short:** 20–25 s target for tips.
4. **Series label + teaser** ("1-qism", "keyingi videoda…") so people follow for the next one.
5. **A share/save line** near the end ("Buni IELTS topshirayotgan do'stingizga yuboring").

From the **meme / educational videos** chat:
6. **On-screen captions on everything.** Many people watch muted.
7. **Follow call tied to future value** in the last 3–5 s ("Har kuni bitta xato — obuna bo'ling"), not just "obuna bo'ling".

From the **photo and certificates** chat:
8. **Hide ID and reference numbers** on any certificate shown (yours or students'). Check the intro reel's certificate shots too.
9. **One surname spelling everywhere.** The IELTS form says "Bakhriddinov", the national certificate says "Bahriddinov". The reel uses **Bakhriddinov**, so keep that.
10. **Telegram captions are limited to 1,024 characters.** Long bilingual text goes in a separate message.

From the **IELTS reel / Telegram video** chats:
11. **Loudness:** master to about **-14 to -16 LUFS**. The intro reel measures -14.5, which is good. Keep music at least 20 dB under your voice.
12. **Logo placement:** keep the Bakhriddinov School logo in one fixed corner.

From the **intro reel itself** (house style, keep it):
13. White label boxes with black uppercase text for facts ("WEBSTER UNIVERSITY · TESOL").
14. Word-by-word captions in the lower third, with the active word highlighted in a box.
15. Number cards for scores (9.0 / 8.5 / 7.0 / 7.0).
16. End on the Bakhriddinov School screen + "+ OBUNA BO'LING".

New rules:
17. **Numbers must agree.** The intro reel shows "44+ o'quvchi" while the caption says "100 dan ortiq" at the same moment. Pick the true one and use it everywhere.
18. **Student faces and results only with their permission** (or a parent's for under-18s, like the 14-year-old).

## 4. Recording guide (tripod, lamp, mic, LOG)

**Camera**
- **Vertical 9:16, 4K if possible.** I crop and reframe, so leave a little space above your head.
- **25 fps with shutter 1/50.** Power in Uzbekistan is 50 Hz, and many LED lamps flicker on camera at 30 fps or 1/60. If you must use 30 fps, test the lamp first.
- **LOG:** expose slightly brighter than looks normal (LOG looks grey and flat; that's correct). Lock **white balance** to match the lamp (usually 5600 K) and lock exposure/focus so it doesn't drift.
- **Camera: Samsung Galaxy S25, recording in Samsung Log.** Grading starts with Samsung's official Samsung Log → Rec.709 conversion LUT, then color-grade-ai's corrective LUT. Samsung Log is turned on in **Pro Video** mode. Use the main 1x lens, which is the sharpest and handles low light best.

**Light**
- Lamp at about 45° to one side, slightly above eye level. Window or wall light as a soft fill on the other side. No strong light behind you.

**Sound**
- Mic 15–20 cm from your mouth, out of frame if possible.
- Before you start talking: **5 seconds of silence** (I use it to clean the noise) and **one clap** (to sync the mic if it records separately).
- Turn off fans and air conditioning.

**Performance**
- Say each line **twice**. I keep the better take and cut the rest, so mistakes don't matter.
- Look into the lens, not the screen.
- Record extra **B-roll**: writing on the board, a student working, your desk, a certificate close-up, walking into the school. 3–5 s each.

**Sending footage**
- Upload to one Google Drive folder per video, named like `2026-11-20_ep01_xato`.
- Set sharing to **"Anyone with the link → Viewer"** and send me the link. (Drive download is now allowed in your cloud environment.)
- Upload the original files, not ones re-sent through Telegram (Telegram compresses them).

## 5. Editing pipeline (what each of your repos does)

| Step | Repo | What it does for you |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Cut | **video-use** | Transcribes, removes "umm" and pauses, picks the best take, adds 30 ms audio fades at every cut, checks every cut before showing you |
| 2. LOG → normal | **color-grade-ai** | Detects LOG, applies your camera's conversion LUT, then a corrective LUT. Matches colour between shoots on different days |
| 3. Look | **video-use** (`grade.py`) + **FFmpeg** | Final look per segment, e.g. warm, clean skin tones |
| 4. Captions | **video-use** / **remotion** | Word-by-word captions in the intro reel's style |
| 5. Graphics | **remotion**, **hyperframes**, **bang-motion** | Score cards, "5.5 → 7.5" animations, lower thirds, kinetic text hooks |
| 6. Templates & music | **claude-code-video-toolkit** | Brand kit, reusable templates, music and sound effects |
| 7. Explainer inserts (optional) | **anything2explainer**, **vox-director** | Short animated explanations inside a reel. vox-director needs a paid Atlas Cloud key |
| 8. Long → short | **openshorts** | If you record a long lesson or live stream, cuts it into several Reels |
| 9. Trend research | **Agent-Reach**, **yt-dlp** | Download reference Reels and read their transcripts to copy formats, not content |
| 10. Covers | **Claude-Image-Production-Plugin**, **fabric.js** | Thumbnails / grid covers |

Not useful for video: **hooks** (a React code library), **claude-mem** (chat memory), **SkillSpector**, and the **lemo-opuscar** repo. **jsoncut-skill** works only through a paid JsonCut API.

**Transcription for Uzbek:** video-use expects an ElevenLabs key for transcription. Without one I can use a free local model, which is less accurate in Uzbek, so I'll check captions against your audio and you should proofread them.

## 6. First 5 scripts (Uzbek, talking head)

Please correct my Uzbek. Say it your way; the structure matters more than the exact words.

### Ep 1 — Pillar A: "I am agree" (25 s)
- **0–2 s (hook text: "10 TA O'ZBEKDAN 9 TASI SHU XATONI QILADI")**: "Siz ham shunday deysizmi? *I am agree.*"
- **2–8 s**: "Bu xato. *Agree* — fe'l. Oldiga *am* kerak emas. To'g'risi: *I agree.*"
- **8–18 s**: "Yana ikkitasi: *make a photo* emas — *take a photo*. *I have 20 years* emas — *I am 20.*"
- **18–25 s**: "Bu xatolar Speaking'da ballingizni tushiradi. Har kuni bitta xato — obuna bo'ling. Buni do'stingizga yuboring."

### Ep 2 — Pillar B: Quiz (30 s)
- **0–2 s (text: "10 SONIYADA JAVOB BERING")**: "Speaking Part 1 savoli: *Do you like your hometown?*"
- **2–20 s**: Show 3 answers on screen, A, B and C. Read each one. "Qaysi biri 5.0, qaysi biri 6.5, qaysi biri 8.0?"
- **20–30 s**: "Javobni kommentda yozing. Ertaga to'g'ri javob va nima uchunligini ko'rsataman."
(Ep 3 is the answer video: replies to comments → a ready-made series.)

### Ep 3 — Pillar C: Student transformation (40 s)
- **0–2 s (text: "5.5 → 7.5")**: Certificate close-up, ID numbers hidden.
- **2–10 s**: "Bu o'quvchim 2 oyda 5.5 dan 7.5 ga chiqdi. U nima qildi?"
- **10–32 s**: "3 ta narsani o'zgartirdi: 1) … 2) … 3) …" (real reasons, with B-roll of the student working).
- **32–40 s**: "Keyingi videoda Writing'da nima qilganini ko'rsataman. Obuna bo'ling."

### Ep 4 — Pillar D: Myth (25 s)
- **0–2 s (text: "8.0 UCHUN QIYIN SO'ZLAR SHART EMAS")**
- **2–18 s**: "Ko'pchilik *ubiquitous*, *plethora* yodlaydi. Lekin imtihonchi to'g'ri va tabiiy ishlatilgan so'zga ball beradi. Masalan: …" (one real example: an over-fancy sentence vs a natural one).
- **18–25 s**: "Saqlab qo'ying, imtihondan oldin kerak bo'ladi."

### Ep 5 — Pillar E: "IELTS ustozining bir kuni" (40 s, timestamped routine)
- **0–2 s (text: "06:30 — IELTS 8.0 USTOZINING BIR KUNI")**
- Then 6–8 short clips, each with a time stamp: 06:30 waking/coffee · 08:00 Webster lecture · 12:00 checking essays · 15:00 class · 19:00 recording this video · 22:00 planning.
- **End**: "Har kuni o'quvchilarimdan nimani o'rganayotganimni shu yerda ko'rsataman."
