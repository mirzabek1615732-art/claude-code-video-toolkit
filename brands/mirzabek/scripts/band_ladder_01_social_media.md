# Band Ladder 01: "Bitta savol, besh xil javob" (Pillar B: Quiz, ladder format)

**Reference format:** a creator stacks five IELTS levels (5.0 to 9.0) on screen and says the same idea five times,
each better than the last. The phrases fill in under each level as he speaks. This script uses the *format* only. All
content, wording and look below are original.

**Format:** one person, one spot, talking head. Five answers to **one** Speaking Part 1 question, from basic to
natural. **Length:** about 30 s. **Goal:** rewatches (people replay to hear the difference), comments (they write which
level they speak at) and sends.
**Why it works:** the viewer *hears* the jump from 5.0 to 9.0, so it teaches without a lecture. The empty 9.0 card
sits on screen from the start, which makes people wait for it.

> Please correct my Uzbek lines. Keep the English answers exactly as written: they are built to sound like five
> different levels.

---

## The question

**"Do you often use social media?"** (Part 1, a topic your audience lives in)

## The five answers (say them exactly, with the acting notes)

| Level | You say | How you say it | What changes at this level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5.0** | "Yes. I use Instagram. It is good." | Flat, short, a little hesitant, eyes up | Very short, simple, no reason given |
| **6.0** | "Yes, every day, because it is interesting." | Steadier, one small pause | Gives a reason with *because* |
| **7.0** | "Yes, quite a lot, mainly to keep in touch with friends." | Smoother, natural rhythm | Flexible phrases ("quite a lot", "keep in touch") |
| **8.0** | "Honestly, more than I'd like to admit, but it keeps me connected." | Relaxed, a half smile | Opinion plus contrast, sounds personal |
| **9.0** | "Absolutely, it's second nature. I just try not to let the hours vanish." | Warm, easy, no effort | Natural idiom, easy flow, a small insight |

The lesson underneath: **higher levels are not harder words, they are more natural English with a reason and a
detail.** (Same lesson as Quiz 01, so the series feels connected.)

## Script (timing for the edit)

### 0:00 to 0:03 · Hook (look into the lens, fast)
**You:** "Bitta savol, besh xil javob. 5.0 dan 9.0 gacha. Farqni eshityapsizmi?"
*(One question, five answers. From 5.0 to 9.0. Can you hear the difference?)*
**On screen (first frame):** the question at the top: `DO YOU OFTEN USE SOCIAL MEDIA?`
**Edit:** the five level cards slide in one after another, last one is `9.0`, still empty.

### 0:03 to 0:23 · The ladder (each level: card lights up, the answer fills in under it)
**You:** say each answer above, in order, with a clear half-second pause between levels.
**Edit per level:** the card flips to white while you speak, the answer text appears under it word by word,
a rising `ding` plays on the card (one note higher each level). On 9.0 a short `riser` leads into an `impact`.

### 0:23 to 0:30 · Call to action (to camera)
**You:** "Siz qaysi darajada gapirasiz? Kommentda raqamini yozing: 5, 6, 7, 8 yoki 9. Har kuni yangi daraja: obuna bo'ling."
*(Which level do you speak at? Write the number in the comments: 5, 6, 7, 8 or 9. A new ladder every day: follow.)*
**On screen:** `5? 6? 7? 8? 9?` then `Har kuni yangi daraja: obuna bo'ling` + the Bakhriddinov School end frame.

## Honesty note (put it in the caption)

IELTS gives one Speaking band for the whole test, not for one answer. These are examples of a *level of English*, not
a score. Caption line: **"Bu ball emas, daraja misoli. IELTS bahosi butun suhbatga beriladi."**
(This is a level example, not a score. IELTS marks the whole conversation.)

## Recording guide (this format)

- **Framing:** stand a little to the **right** of the frame and leave the left side and the top clear. The ladder
  sits on the left. Record in **4K**: I can slide the crop to put you exactly where the cards need space.
- Chest-up, into the lens, lamp from the side, mic out of frame.
- Record the **five answers as five separate takes**, then the hook and the call to action. Say each take **twice**.
- **Acting is the whole video:** 5.0 slow and flat, 9.0 warm and easy. Don't rush the weak levels, the contrast is
  what people replay.
- 5 s of silence at the start of the file, one clap.
- Don't wear the same clothes as the reference creator: you are original. Your usual jacket works.
