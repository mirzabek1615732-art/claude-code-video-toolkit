# Mistake 01: "Xato politsiyasi" — I am agree (Pillar A: Uzbek mistakes)

**Format:** comedy skit, two characters, both played by you. A student keeps making mistakes; the "Mistake Police"
blows a whistle and stops him each time.
**Length:** 28–30 s. **Goal:** sends ("this is my friend 😂") and rewatches (the ending loops into the start).
**Series label:** `XATO #1`. Every Mistake Police episode uses the same characters, whistle and sounds.

> Please correct my Uzbek. Keep the structure, the jokes and the timing; say the lines your way.

---

## Characters

| Who | Look | Acting | On-screen tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Student "Aziz"** | Cap, notebook | Confident, cheerful, *very* sure he's right | — |
| **Mistake Police (you)** | Your jacket + a whistle (and a toy badge if you have one) | Strict, deadpan, then a small smile at the joke | `XATO POLITSIYASI 🚨` (first time only) |

Film each character as separate takes in the same spot; I cut them as a conversation (shot / reverse shot).

## Script

### 0:00–0:02 · Hook (the mistake *is* the hook)
**Aziz (proudly, to camera):** "*I am agree!*"
**SFX:** whistle 🔊 → freeze frame + red ❌ stamp + `impact`
**On screen (big, first frame already shows):** `BU XATONI KO'PCHILIK QILADI` *(Most people make this mistake)*
**Label (corner, whole video):** `XATO #1`

### 0:02–0:09 · Mistake 1: I am agree
**Police (deadpan):** "To'xta! Sen 'agree' emassan, sen Azizsan. 😐"
*(Stop! You're not an "agree", you're Aziz.)*
**Police (explains, quick):** "*Agree* — fe'l. Oldiga *am* kerak emas. To'g'risi: ***I agree.***"
*(Agree is a verb. No "am" before it. Correct: I agree.)*
**On screen:** `❌ I am agree` → `✅ I agree`
**SFX:** `wrong_buzzer` on ❌, `correct` on ✅, `whoosh_scene_change_1` back to Aziz.

### 0:09–0:16 · Mistake 2: make a photo
**Aziz (holding up phone, happy):** "Teacher, can I *make a photo*?"
**SFX:** whistle 🔊 + freeze
**Police:** "Rasmni o'zing chizmoqchimisan? 🎨 Inglizlar rasmni ***take*** qiladi: ***take a photo.***"
*(Are you going to draw the photo yourself? In English you take a photo.)*
**On screen:** `❌ make a photo` → `✅ take a photo`
**SFX:** `wrong_buzzer`, `camera_shutter` on ✅ (instead of `correct`: a small surprise), `swoosh_fast_1` back.

### 0:16–0:23 · Mistake 3: I have 20 years
**Aziz (proud):** "*I have 20 years.*"
**SFX:** whistle 🔊🔊 (two blasts, louder: escalation) + freeze + quick zoom on Police's face
**Police (shocked):** "20 yil?! Qamoqda o'tirganmisan?! 😳"
*(20 years?! Were you in prison?!)*
**Police (calm again):** "Yosh *be* bilan aytiladi: ***I am 20.***"
*(Age goes with "be": I am 20.)*
**On screen:** `❌ I have 20 years` → `✅ I am 20`
**SFX:** `wrong_buzzer`, `correct`.

### 0:23–0:26 · Payoff + loop
**Aziz (defeated, small voice):** "Okay… *I agree.*"
**Police (finally smiles, thumbs up):** "Ana! 👏"
**SFX:** `ding`
*(The video ends on "I agree", and replays from "I am agree": the loop makes people rewatch.)*

### 0:26–0:30 · Call to action (Police, to camera)
**Police:** "Sen qaysi birini qilarding? Kommentda yoz. Bu videoni 'I am agree' deydigan do'stingga yubor 😅.
Har kuni bitta xato: obuna bo'l."
*(Which one did you make? Write in the comments. Send this to your friend who says "I am agree". One mistake a day:
follow.)*
**On screen:** `Har kuni 1 ta xato ➜ obuna bo'ling` + Bakhriddinov School end frame (house style).
**SFX:** `pop_text` on the follow text.

## Notes

- **Hook claim:** the old version said "10 tadan 9 tasi" (9 out of 10). We have no data for that number, so the hook
  now says **"ko'pchilik"** (most people). Same punch, no made-up statistic.
- **Sen vs Siz:** the Police uses *sen* in the skit because it's a playful character talking to a student. If you
  prefer *siz* for your brand, change it everywhere; just keep it consistent.
- **Whistle sound:** not in the pack yet. I'll generate one with ElevenLabs (a few credits) or you can record a real
  whistle on set, which is even better.
- **Caption pace:** one ❌/✅ card per mistake, big, centre-screen; your word-by-word captions in the lower third.
