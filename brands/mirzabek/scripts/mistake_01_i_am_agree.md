# Mistake 01: "Xato politsiyasi" — I am agree (Pillar A: Uzbek mistakes)

**Format:** comedy skit, one person, no costumes. You play two roles; each role has its **own background, framing and
posture**, so viewers know who is talking in half a second.
**Length:** 28–30 s. **Goal:** sends ("this is my friend 😂") and rewatches (the ending loops into the start).
**Series label:** `XATO #1`. Every Mistake Police episode reuses the same two spots, so the series looks consistent.

> Please correct my Uzbek. Keep the structure, the jokes and the timing; say the lines your way.

---

## The two roles (same clothes, different places)

| | **Aziz, the student** | **Mistake Police, the teacher** |
|---|---|---|
| **Background** | Spot A: sitting at a desk or table, window or bookshelf behind | Spot B: standing at the whiteboard or the Bakhriddinov School screen |
| **Framing** | Wider: waist up, slightly from the side | Close: chest up, straight into the lens |
| **Posture** | Relaxed, leaning back, big smile, overconfident | Straight, arms crossed, deadpan |
| **Prop** | Your phone (for "make a photo"), a notebook | A marker; raise your hand **flat like a STOP sign** at each mistake |
| **Name tag (first time only)** | `AZIZ` | `XATO POLITSIYASI 🚨` |

The whistle is added in the edit as a sound effect, timed to your STOP hand. No whistle needed on set.

**Filming order:** film *all* of Aziz's lines in Spot A, then move the tripod and film *all* of the Police lines in
Spot B. I cut them together as a conversation. Look slightly **left** as Aziz and slightly **right** as the Police in
the reaction lines, so they seem to face each other.

## Script

### 0:00–0:02 · Hook (the mistake *is* the hook) · Spot A
**Aziz (proudly, to camera):** "*I am agree!*"
**Cut to Spot B:** Police, hand up like a STOP sign.
**SFX:** whistle 🔊 → freeze frame + red ❌ stamp + `impact`
**On screen (big, from the first frame):** `BU XATONI KO'PCHILIK QILADI` *(Most people make this mistake)*
**Label (corner, whole video):** `XATO #1`

### 0:02–0:09 · Mistake 1: I am agree · Spot B
**Police (deadpan):** "To'xta! Sen 'agree' emassan, sen Azizsan. 😐"
*(Stop! You're not an "agree", you're Aziz.)*
**Police (explains, quick):** "*Agree* — fe'l. Oldiga *am* kerak emas. To'g'risi: ***I agree.***"
*(Agree is a verb. No "am" before it. Correct: I agree.)*
**On screen:** `❌ I am agree` → `✅ I agree`
**SFX:** `wrong_buzzer` on ❌, `correct` on ✅, whoosh back to Spot A.

### 0:09–0:16 · Mistake 2: make a photo · Spot A → B
**Aziz (holding up his phone, happy):** "Teacher, can I *make a photo*?"
**Cut to Spot B:** STOP hand. **SFX:** whistle 🔊 + freeze
**Police:** "Rasmni o'zing chizmoqchimisan? 🎨 Inglizlar rasmni ***take*** qiladi: ***take a photo.***"
*(Are you going to draw the photo yourself? In English you take a photo.)*
**On screen:** `❌ make a photo` → `✅ take a photo`
**SFX:** `wrong_buzzer`, `camera_shutter` on ✅ (a small surprise), swoosh back.

### 0:16–0:23 · Mistake 3: I have 20 years · Spot A → B
**Aziz (proud):** "*I have 20 years.*"
**Cut to Spot B:** a quick punch-in zoom on the Police's shocked face. **SFX:** whistle 🔊🔊 (two blasts: escalation)
**Police (shocked):** "20 yil?! Qamoqda o'tirganmisan?! 😳"
*(20 years?! Were you in prison?!)*
**Police (calm again):** "Yosh *be* bilan aytiladi: ***I am 20.***"
*(Age goes with "be": I am 20.)*
**On screen:** `❌ I have 20 years` → `✅ I am 20`
**SFX:** `wrong_buzzer`, `correct`.

### 0:23–0:26 · Payoff + loop · Spot A → B
**Aziz (defeated, sinking in his chair, small voice):** "Okay… *I agree.*"
**Spot B — Police (finally smiles, thumbs up):** "Ana! 👏"
**SFX:** `ding`
*(The video ends on "I agree" and replays from "I am agree": the loop makes people rewatch.)*

### 0:26–0:30 · Call to action · Spot B
**Police (to camera):** "Sen qaysi birini qilarding? Kommentda yoz. Bu videoni 'I am agree' deydigan do'stingga
yubor 😅. Har kuni bitta xato: obuna bo'l."
*(Which one did you make? Write in the comments. Send this to your friend who says "I am agree". One mistake a day:
follow.)*
**On screen:** `Har kuni 1 ta xato ➜ obuna bo'ling` + Bakhriddinov School end frame (house style).
**SFX:** `pop_text` on the follow text.

## Notes

- **Hook claim:** "ko'pchilik" (most people), not "10 tadan 9 tasi": we have no data for that number.
- **Sen vs Siz:** the Police uses *sen* because it's a playful character talking to a student. If you prefer *siz* for
  your brand, change it everywhere; just keep it consistent.
- **Whistle sound:** not in the pack yet. I'll generate one with ElevenLabs (a few credits).
- **Editing helps the two roles read:** Spot A gets a slightly warmer colour grade, Spot B slightly cooler. A small
  difference, but it tells the eye "different person".
- **Caption pace:** one ❌/✅ card per mistake, big, centre-screen; word-by-word captions in the lower third.
