# Quiz 01: "Qaysi biri 8.0?" (Pillar B: Quiz)

**Format:** mini game show. You play the host *and* three students, each with one prop.
**Length:** 32–36 s. **Goal:** comments (people argue which answer is 8.0) and sends ("test your friend").
**Series:** this is part 1. Part 2 (the answer) is posted the next day and replies to the best comment.

**The twist that drives comments:** most viewers will pick the student with the fancy words. The real 8.0 is the
natural one. Don't reveal it in this video.

> Please correct my Uzbek. Keep the structure and timing; say the lines your way.

## Script

### 0:00–0:03 · Hook
**On screen (first frame, big):** `QAYSI BIRI 8.0? 🤔`
**Host (to camera, fast):** "Bitta savol, uchta o'quvchi. Qaysi biri **8.0** oladi? Oxirigacha ko'ring!"
*(One question, three students. Which one gets 8.0? Watch to the end!)*
**SFX:** `riser` under the line → `impact` on "8.0".

### 0:03–0:06 · The question
**On screen:** `Speaking Part 1: "Do you like your hometown?"`
**Host (as examiner, mock-serious, holding a pen):** "*Do you like your hometown?*"
**SFX:** `ding` when the question text appears.

### 0:06–0:12 · Student A (cap)
**On screen tag:** `A`
**Student A (nervous):** "Ehm… yes. I like my hometown. Because… it is very beautiful. And people is very kind."
**Edit:** quick zoom on his face at "people is". No buzzer yet: no hints!
**SFX:** `whoosh_scene_change_3` into the shot.

### 0:12–0:20 · Student B (glasses + tie)
**On screen tag:** `B`
**Student B (slow, finger raised):** "Indubitably. My hometown is an *unparalleled* metropolis, which *plethora* of *magnificent*
historical monuments *embellish*."
**Edit:** slow push-in, dramatic. Optional tiny "✨" sticker on "unparalleled".
**SFX:** `whoosh_scene_change_2` in; `bubble_pop` on the sticker.

### 0:20–0:26 · Student C (no prop)
**On screen tag:** `C`
**Student C (relaxed, smiling):** "Yeah, I really do. It's where I grew up, so it has a special place in my heart. It's
not the most exciting place, but people there really look out for each other."
**SFX:** `whoosh_scene_change_1` in.

### 0:26–0:32 · Call to action (host)
**On screen:** `A?  B?  C?` (three boxes pop in one by one)
**Host:** "Xo'sh, qaysi biri 8.0? **Kommentda harf bilan yozing.** Ertaga javobni va **nega**ligini ko'rsataman."
*(So which one is 8.0? Write the letter in the comments. Tomorrow I'll show the answer and why.)*
**SFX:** `pop_text` on each box.

### 0:32–0:35 · Send + follow
**On screen:** `Do'stingizni sinab ko'ring 👀`
**Host (smirking):** "IELTS topshirayotgan do'stingizga yuboring, u ham topa olarmikan? Javobni o'tkazib yubormang: obuna bo'ling."
*(Send it to a friend taking IELTS, let's see if they get it. Don't miss the answer: follow.)*
**End frame:** Bakhriddinov School screen + `+ OBUNA BO'LING` (house style).

## Part 2 (next day): the answer, 30 s

1. **Hook (0–2 s):** reply-sticker of the top comment saying "B" + text `KO'PCHILIK XATO QILDI 😅`
   *(Most people got it wrong)*.
2. **A ≈ 5.0–5.5:** `wrong_buzzer`. Two grammar errors: "people **is**" → "people **are**", and "Because…" as a separate
   sentence. Very short answer.
3. **B ≈ 5.5–6.0:** `wrong_buzzer`. Big words used **wrongly** and in the wrong style ("plethora … embellish" isn't
   grammatical; "metropolis" for a small town). Examiners can also tell a memorised answer, and memorised language
   doesn't count as your own.
4. **C ≈ 7.5–8.0:** `correct` + `impact`. Answers directly, gives a reason, natural phrases ("a special place in my
   heart", "look out for each other"), honest contrast ("not the most exciting place, but…").
5. **Lesson + CTA:** "8.0 = qiyin so'z emas, **tabiiy** ingliz tili. Saqlab qo'ying." *(8.0 isn't hard words, it's
   natural English. Save this.)*

**Be careful with the claim:** band scores are given for the whole test, not one answer. Say "taxminan" (about) or
"darajasida" (at the level of), never "this answer gets exactly 8.0".
