# Mistake 01 (explainer): 3 mistakes Uzbeks make in English (Pillar A)

**Format:** talking head, one person, one spot. Teaching, not acting. (The skit version, `mistake_01_i_am_agree.md`,
is saved for next week.)
**Length:** 28–32 s. **Goal:** saves and sends.
**Spot:** standing or sitting in front of the whiteboard or the Bakhriddinov School screen, chest-up, into the lens.
**Series label:** `XATO #1` in the corner.

> Please correct my Uzbek. Keep the structure and the timing; say the lines your way.

---

## How it stays engaging without a skit

- **Open loop:** you promise that the 3rd mistake is the funniest, so people stay to the end.
- **Something changes every 2–3 s:** a punch-in zoom, a ❌/✅ card, a sound. No 5-second stretch of the same frame.
- **Counting:** "1… 2… 3…" gives the viewer a map and makes the video feel short.
- **Your face does the work:** a raised eyebrow at each mistake, a smile at the fix.

## Script

### 0:00–0:03 · Hook
**You (straight into the lens, slightly fast):** "Bu 3 ta xatoni deyarli hamma o'zbek qiladi. ***Uchinchisi*** eng kulgilisi."
*(Almost every Uzbek makes these 3 mistakes. The third one is the funniest.)*
**On screen (first frame, big):** `3 TA XATO 🚫` and `3-chisi eng kulgili 😅` (small, under it)
**SFX:** `impact` on "3 ta xato".
**Edit:** start already mid-sentence, no breath at the start.

### 0:03–0:11 · Mistake 1: I am agree
**You:** "**Birinchi:** *I am agree.* ❌ *Agree* — fe'l. Fe'l oldidan *am* kerak emas. Shunchaki: ***I agree.*** ✅"
*(First: "I am agree". Agree is a verb. No "am" before a verb. Just: I agree.)*
**On screen:** `1` (big number pops) → `❌ I am agree` → `✅ I agree`
**SFX:** `pop_text` on "1", `wrong_buzzer` on ❌, `correct` on ✅.
**Edit:** punch-in zoom on "fe'l".

### 0:11–0:18 · Mistake 2: make a photo
**You:** "**Ikkinchi:** *make a photo.* ❌ Inglizlar rasmni *qilmaydi*, *oladi*: ***take a photo.*** ✅"
*(Second: "make a photo". English speakers don't "make" a photo, they "take" one: take a photo.)*
**Hand gesture:** mime taking a photo with your hands on "take".
**On screen:** `2` → `❌ make a photo` → `✅ take a photo`
**SFX:** `swoosh_fast_1` in, `wrong_buzzer`, `camera_shutter` on ✅.

### 0:18–0:26 · Mistake 3: I have 20 years (the payoff)
**You (small smile):** "**Uchinchi**, eng kulgilisi: *I have 20 years.* ❌ Inglizga bu '20 yil qamoq' kabi eshitiladi 😅.
Yosh ***be*** bilan aytiladi: ***I am 20.*** ✅"
*(Third, the funniest: "I have 20 years". To an English speaker it sounds like "20 years in prison". Age goes with
"be": I am 20.)*
**On screen:** `3` → `❌ I have 20 years` → `✅ I am 20`
**SFX:** `whoosh_scene_change_3` in, `wrong_buzzer`, `ding` on ✅.
**Edit:** quick zoom-in on your face on "qamoq", then back.

### 0:26–0:31 · Call to action
**You:** "Qaysi birini siz ham qilardingiz? Kommentda raqamini yozing. Har kuni bitta xato: **obuna bo'ling**, va buni
do'stingizga yuboring."
*(Which one did you make? Write its number in the comments. One mistake a day: follow, and send this to a friend.)*
**On screen:** `1? 2? 3? 👇` then `Har kuni 1 ta xato ➜ obuna bo'ling` + Bakhriddinov School end frame.
**SFX:** `pop_text` on each number.

## Notes

- **"20 years in prison" joke:** keep it light ("kabi eshitiladi", *sounds like*). Native speakers would understand
  you; the point is that it sounds odd, not that it's wrong in every context.
- **Comment prompt is a number (1, 2, 3):** easier to type than a sentence, so more people comment.
- **Hook claim:** "deyarli hamma" (almost everyone) is a figure of speech, not a statistic. If you'd rather be safer,
  say "ko'pchilik" (most people).
