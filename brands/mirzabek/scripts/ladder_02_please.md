# Ladder 02: "Iltimos"ning 5 xil yo'li, B1 dan C2 gacha (Pillar B: ladder format)

**Same format as Ladder 01** (five levels stacked on screen, answers fill in as you speak), new idea: five ways to ask
people to follow your page. The fifth way is your Uzbek line, and it is the punchline.
**Length:** about 28 s. **Goal:** comments (the comment word is **"oka"**), shares and follows. The video asks for the
follow *as the content*, so the call to action feels like part of the joke.

> Please correct my Uzbek lines. The English lines are final. The Uzbek line on the fifth card is exactly yours.

---

## The joke in one sentence

CEFR stops at C2, so for the fifth card there is no English level left. You drop the formal English and use the
Uzbek one that actually works: **"Bitta padderjka qilvoring endi oka."**
*(Give me one bit of support now, bro. In this context: one follow.)*

## The five cards

| Card | You say | How you say it | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1 daraja** | "Please follow my page." | Stiff, a bit nervous, hands together | Short and direct, just *please* |
| **B2 daraja** | "Could you follow my page, please?" | Polite, a small smile | A modal (*could*) makes it softer |
| **C1 daraja** | "I'd really appreciate it if you could follow my page." | Calm, professional, steady eye contact | A full polite structure, shows the reason |
| **C2 daraja** | "It would mean the world to me if you gave my page a follow." | Warm, hand on chest, slow | Natural idiom (*mean the world*, *give a follow*), personal |
| **O'zbek daraja** | "Bitta padderjka qilvoring endi oka." | **Switch completely:** lean into the lens, one hand out like begging, a wink | The strongest ask. The contrast with C2 is the laugh |

Note for the lesson underneath: asking gets *warmer and more natural* as the level goes up, not longer. C2 is
shorter than people expect.

## Script (timing for the edit)

### 0:00 to 0:04 · Hook (into the lens, fast)
**You:** "Obuna so'rashning 5 xil yo'li: B1 dan C2 gacha. Oxirgisi eng kuchlisi 😅"
*(Five ways to ask for a follow: B1 to C2. The last one is the strongest.)*
**On screen (first frame):** `5 WAYS TO SAY "PLEASE FOLLOW"`
**Edit:** the five cards slide in one after another; the fifth, `O'ZBEK DARAJASI`, stays empty.

### 0:04 to 0:19 · The four English levels
Say each line, half a second of pause between levels, and **change your posture each time** (stiff, polite, calm, warm).
**Edit per level:** the card flips to white while you speak, the line appears under it word by word, a rising `ding`.

### 0:19 to 0:22 · The Uzbek level (the punchline)
1. After the C2 line, **stop for a full second** and look at the camera. The `riser` builds in this silence.
2. **Drop the formal posture.** Lean in, hand out, wink: "**Bitta padderjka qilvoring endi oka.**"
3. **Edit:** `impact` on "Bitta", the fifth card flips to white, the line appears word by word. Add a quick punch-in zoom
   on "oka".

### 0:22 to 0:28 · Call to action (back to normal posture)
**You:** "Siz qaysi biri bilan so'rardingiz? Kommentda yozing: B1, B2, C1, C2 yoki **'oka'** 😄 Obuna bo'ling, iltimos."
*(Which one would you use? Write it in the comments: B1, B2, C1, C2 or "oka". Follow, please.)*
**On screen:** `B1  B2  C1  C2  OKA` as five round buttons, then `OBUNA BO'LING, ILTIMOS`.

## Honesty note (put it in the caption)

CEFR levels are assessed across many tasks, not from one sentence. These are examples of *register* (how formal and
natural a request sounds), not a certified level. Caption line: **"Bu CEFR bahosi emas, uslub misoli."**
*(This is not a CEFR assessment, it is a style example.)*

## Recording guide (same as Ladder 01, plus this)

- Stand a little to the **right**, leave the left and top clear for the ladder. Record in **4K**.
- Film each of the five lines as its own take, **twice**, and the hook and call to action once each.
- **The acting is the video.** Make the B1 and the Uzbek line as different as you can: stiff and nervous, then relaxed
  and cheeky.
- Hold the silence before the Uzbek line for a full second on camera. I'll cut it to the right length.
- 5 s of silence at the start of the file, one clap.
