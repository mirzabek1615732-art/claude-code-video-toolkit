Fact check: Venus turns once relative to the distant stars (a sidereal rotation) in about 243 Earth days, and it spins backwards (retrograde). It orbits the Sun in about 225 Earth days. Its solar day, sunrise to sunrise, is shorter: about 117 Earth days. So one full rotation is longer than its year, but its day (sunrise to sunrise) is not. The video says 'rotation' throughout and shows the 117-day note on screen.
