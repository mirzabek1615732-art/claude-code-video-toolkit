EPISODE 5: YOUR BRAIN IS HIDING SOMETHING!  (Bakhriddinov School)

Target expression: I CAN'T UNSEE IT!
  Meaning: once you have noticed something (strange, funny or awkward), you can't stop noticing it.
  Uzbek: Endi buni ko‘rmaganga ololmayman!
  When to say it: a friend points out something odd in a picture, a logo or a face you can't stop seeing, and you say "I can't unsee it!"

Fact check: your nose sits inside your field of view, but you normally don't notice it. Your eyes still see it; the brain treats this constant, unchanging detail as unimportant (selective attention and adaptation to unchanging input), so it rarely reaches awareness. Paying attention to it makes it noticeable again, and how much of it you see differs from person to person. The video shows the brain 'hiding' it only as a metaphor.
