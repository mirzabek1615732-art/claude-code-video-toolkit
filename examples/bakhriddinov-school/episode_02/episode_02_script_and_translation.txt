EPISODE 2: WHY ARE FLAMINGOS PINK?  (Bakhriddinov School)

Target expression: YOU ARE WHAT YOU EAT
  Meaning: the food you eat affects your body and health.
  Uzbek: Yegan ovqating tanang va sog‘lig‘ingga ta’sir qiladi.
Secondary word: PIGMENT
  Uzbek: Rang beruvchi modda.

NARRATION (English) with Uzbek translation and timing

[00.35-02.97] Wait! Flamingos aren't born pink.
    UZ 00.24-01.19  Shoshma!
    UZ 01.22-03.25  Flamingolar pushti rangda tug‘ilmaydi.

[03.35-04.39] They're born gray!
    UZ 03.25-04.79  Ular kulrang bo‘lib tug‘iladi!

[05.00-06.48] So, who painted them?
    UZ 04.90-06.90  Unda ularni kim bo‘yagan?

[07.20-08.88] Nobody. Their food did!
    UZ 07.11-08.04  Hech kim.
    UZ 08.07-09.35  Ularni o‘z ovqati bo‘yagan!

[09.60-12.43] Tiny shrimp and algae contain special pigments.
    UZ 09.50-10.94  Mitti krevetkalar va suvo‘tlarda
    UZ 10.97-12.88  maxsus pigmentlar bor.

[13.00-15.73] Over time, these help turn flamingos pink.
    UZ 12.89-13.97  Vaqt o‘tishi bilan
    UZ 14.00-16.19  ular flamingolarni asta-sekin pushti rangga bo‘yaydi.

[16.55-17.70] That's why we say...
    UZ 16.45-18.03  Shuning uchun shunday deymiz:

[18.15-19.48] You are what you eat!
    UZ 18.03-19.93  “Nima yesang, o‘sha bo‘lasan!”

[20.60-22.35] It means your diet affects your body.
    UZ 20.50-22.81  Ya’ni yegan ovqating tanangga ta’sir qiladi.

[24.90-27.48] So... that friend who drinks pink smoothies every day?
    UZ 24.80-26.31  Xo‘sh... har kuni pushti smuzi ichadigan
    UZ 26.34-27.85  o‘sha do‘sting-chi?

[27.95-29.20] Maybe keep an eye on them!
    UZ 27.85-29.63  Unga ko‘z-quloq bo‘lib tur!

Fact check: flamingo chicks hatch grayish-white; carotenoid pigments from algae and small crustaceans in their diet give adults their pink colour over months to years. The smoothie line is a joke: drinks do not turn people pink.
