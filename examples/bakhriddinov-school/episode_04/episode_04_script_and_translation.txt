Target expression: PLOT TWIST!
  Meaning: an unexpected change or surprising development in a story or situation.
  Uzbek: Kutilmagan burilish!

NARRATION (English) with Uzbek translation and timing. The last line is the strawberry's own voice.

Fact check: a botanical berry develops from the single ovary of one flower and has fleshy walls with seeds inside. Bananas fit this (cultivated bananas have tiny undeveloped seeds). Strawberries are aggregate accessory fruits: the red flesh grows from the swollen flower base (the receptacle), and each 'seed' on the outside is an achene, a tiny dry fruit with one seed inside.
